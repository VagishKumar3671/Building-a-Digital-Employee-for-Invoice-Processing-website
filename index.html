01 — The Problem

Finance teams are
drowning in manual work

Every invoice processed by hand is a cost, a risk, and a delay. The current workflow was never designed to scale.

Why This Breaks Down

⟳

Human Error at Scale A typo in vendor matching can result in fraud or duplicate payments
⏱

Build a digital employee that performs invoice processing tasks reliably — understanding invoices, validating information, handling exceptions, and continuously improving through feedback — without requiring a human to babysit every decision.

04 — How The System Works

Every decision is
explainable and auditable

AI handles what it's best at — reading messy documents. Rules handle what compliance demands — predictable, traceable decisions.

Processing Pipeline

Input
Invoice PDF
Any format · Scanned or digital
AI Layer
Extraction Engine
Groq · Llama 3.3 70B · OCR fallback
Rules Layer
13 Validation Checks
Deterministic · Per-check audit trail
Scoring
Confidence Assessment
Itemised breakdown · Not a black box
Decision
Final Status + Email Draft
With suggested action · Full reasoning

Decision Outputs

Approved

All checks passed · Auto-logged · ERP-ready

Flagged for Review

Needs human eyes · Draft email generated · Reason cited

Rejected

Duplicate / unknown vendor / major variance · Hard stop

Validation Checks at a Glance

Invoice Date Sanity
Future-dated or stale (>180 days) → Flag
Critical Fields
Invoice #, vendor name, total amount must exist
Credit Note Detection
Negative amount / CN- prefix / "credit" keyword
Duplicate Detection
Invoice # + vendor fuzzy match at 90% threshold
Vendor Verification
Fuzzy match vendor master (85%) + approval status
PO Matching
Direct lookup → fuzzy inference → closed PO detect
Amount Comparison
<5% → Approve · 5–10% → Flag · >10% → Reject
Cumulative Invoicing
Tracks running total per PO across multiple invoices
🔁

Human overrides are converted into approved learning rules — stored as auditable JSON and applied automatically to all future invoices.

05 — Edge Cases & Reliability

Real systems fail at
the edges — so I started there

I catalogued 16 edge cases across two categories before writing the validation layer. Each one maps to a real financial risk or operational failure mode.

Business Edge Cases
Duplicate Invoice
Risk: double payment
Reject
Vendor Mismatch
Risk: fraud or incorrect billing
Flag
Amount Mismatch >10%
Risk: unauthorised spending
Reject
Closed Purchase Order
Risk: payment against inactive PO
Flag
Future-Dated Invoice
Risk: data entry error
Flag
Unapproved Vendor
Risk: compliance violation
Reject
Technical Edge Cases
OCR extraction errors on scanned PDFs
Corrupted or blank PDF uploads
Low-confidence AI extraction
Partial invoice data — missing non-critical fields
Multiple invoice formats (lump sum vs itemised)
Vendor name abbreviations & typos (fuzzy matching)
Groq API rate limits — model fallback chain
Solution Design: Every technical failure routes to human review with a confidence score and error code — not a silent failure. The system degrades gracefully, never silently.

06 — Business Impact

This is not a tool.
It's a digital employee

The goal was never to automate a checklist. It was to build something that earns more autonomy over time — exactly how Zamp thinks about AI at work.

Operational Impact
Eliminated Repetitive Manual Work Extraction, validation, and decision — fully automated end-to-end. No human touches a normal invoice
Full Per-Check Audit Visibility Every check logged with pass/fail reasoning and machine-readable error codes — not a black box confidence score
Faster Processing Cycles Hours of manual review compressed to seconds. Context-specific draft emails generated for every flagged or rejected invoice
Better Vendor Relationships Faster decisions mean faster vendor payments — reduces late payment risk and improves supplier trust
Business Impact
Scales Without Adding Headcount 3× invoice volume requires zero additional hire. The system processes batch or single invoices identically
Reduces Compliance & Fraud Risk Unapproved vendors, duplicates, closed POs, and amount variances caught systematically — every time
Compounds in Intelligence Over Time Human feedback becomes a permanent approved rule. The system earns more autonomy with every correction cycle
ERP-Ready Output Full audit trail stored as structured JSON — ready to integrate with SAP, Oracle, or NetSuite
Biggest Learning

Successful AI systems are not built around models. They are built around:

→Understanding the workflow deeply before automating
→Designing exception handling before the happy path
→Keeping AI and deterministic rules clearly separated
→Building reliable human-AI collaboration, not replacement
